**Title:** RepairScope: Consensus certain answers over conflicting records

**Notes / Description** (972 characters):

RepairScope is a standalone GenLayer consistent-query oracle for contradictory prose records. A deployment fixes a snapshot, typed rosters and a commit-pinned report/SHA-256. Leader and validators independently fetch full text, classify every record and verify named custody fields and quotes; unresolved assertions block answers. Agreed tuples feed exact enumeration of all maximum-cardinality repairs under two fixed functional dependencies. Duplicate assertions retain provenance without extra weight. Permissionless conjunctive queries return CERTAIN, POSSIBLE or IMPOSSIBLE with witnesses and counterexamples; read-only evaluation survives a full query journal. StudioNet proofs show a certain answer with no certain tuple, correlated joins, maximum-versus-maximal repair selection, duplicate/forecast handling and REVIEW rejection. Includes 58 direct tests, exhaustive/generated oracles and 24 finalized receipts. Synthetic reports do not establish physical custody.
